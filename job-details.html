Senior Product Designer

⌖ New York, NY ＄ $135k – $165k / year ◷ 5+ years experience

About Sunroom

Sunroom is building a more human way to manage money. We believe financial tools should feel clear, calm, and made for real life. Our small, curious team is creating products that help people feel more confident about the choices they make every day.

The opportunity

We’re looking for a Senior Product Designer to make complex financial moments feel simple and welcoming. You’ll partner with product and engineering from early ideas through launch, shaping the experience and raising the bar for craft across our app.

What you’ll do

  • Lead end-to-end design for meaningful product areas, from discovery to polished, shipped experiences.
  • Turn customer needs and business goals into clear flows, prototypes, and thoughtful interface details.
  • Work closely with product managers, engineers, and researchers to explore and deliver solutions.
  • Help evolve our design system and share practices that make the whole team stronger.
  • Use research, data, and your own curiosity to keep improving what we build.

What you bring

  • 5+ years designing digital products, with a portfolio that shows your thinking and craft.
  • Strong interaction and visual design skills across web and mobile experiences.
  • Comfort working through ambiguity and explaining your decisions with care.
  • Experience partnering with product and engineering in a collaborative team.
  • Fluency with Figma and a healthy appetite for feedback and iteration.
